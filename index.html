Nokia 3310 & Friendster
Social Domain SMS CLANS & OFWS

The Texting Capital to 5G Feeds

Evolution from 160-character SMS clans and early OFW video calls to instant global feeds.

2000–2010 Decade

Nokia 3310 keypad texting, SMS clans, Friendster testimonials, and Yahoo Messenger video calls for OFW families.

Present Time

5G mobile smartphones, TikTok algorithms, Instagram Reels, Messenger, and live-streaming online communities.

Impact: Birthed the hyper-connected Pinoy netizen
OPM Rock & P-Pop
Cultural Domain MP3 CDS & FANTASERYES

OPM Band Craze to Global P-Pop

From burned MP3 CDs and SexBomb dance crazes to global streaming and fandoms.

2000–2010 Decade

OPM rock explosion (Parokya ni Edgar, Kamikazee), Quiapo burned MP3 CDs, SexBomb dance hits, and primetime fantaseryes (*Mulawin*).

Present Time

P-Pop international wave (SB19, BINI), Spotify streaming royalties, TikTok dance challenges, and Netflix adaptations.

Impact: Decentralized creator economy & global fandom
Pacquiao & LAN Shops
Sports & Cyberculture DOTA 1 & CRIME DROPS

LAN Cafes & Fight Days to Olympic Gold

From empty streets during Manny Pacquiao fights and DotA 1 cafes to multi-sport triumphs and global Esports.

2000–2010 Decade

Zero crime rate during Pacquiao boxing broadcasts, PBA arena finals, and bulky CRT computer shops playing DotA 1 and Counter-Strike.

Present Time

Olympic gold medals (Carlos Yulo, Hidilyn Diaz), professional Esports championships (MPL/MLBB), and mobile game streaming.

Impact: LAN culture built the foundation for PH Esports dominance
EDSA II & Text Brigades
Political Domain GMS & HELLO GARCI

Text Revolutions to Algorithmic Politics

How SMS chain messages mobilized EDSA Dos, contrasted with modern digital video campaigning.

2000–2010 Decade

"Go 2 EDSA, wear black" SMS chains that toppled Estrada, the Hello Garci scandal, and political satire shows (*Wazzup Wazzup*).

Present Time

TikTok viral campaigns, political podcasts, algorithmic influencer endorsements, and live-streamed hearings.

Impact: SMS chains birthed viral citizen mobilization
BPO Nightshifts & Piracy
Economic Domain CALL CENTERS & DVDS

The Call Center Boom to the Gig Economy

Transition from 24/7 BPO nightshift economies and pirated DVDs to cashless e-wallets.

2000–2010 Decade

Explosion of call centers creating the 24/7 night economy, Quiapo pirated DVD stalls, and TV rating wars.

Present Time

Cashless e-wallets (GCash, Maya), platform e-commerce (Shopee, TikTok Shop), and digital content monetization.

Impact: BPO nightshifts established the 24/7 consumer society
Typhoon Ondoy (2009)
Environmental Domain DISASTER RESCUE TWEETS

Disaster Alerts & Online Rescue Mobilization

How Typhoon Ondoy in 2009 transformed emergency communication from television to online coordination.

2000–2010 Decade

Broadcast TV and radio storm signals; Typhoon Ondoy (2009) prompted the first viral online rescue calls and donation drives.

Present Time

Real-time mobile weather apps, crowd-sourced flood rescue maps, and social media youth climate advocacy.

Impact: Sparked community-driven real-time disaster response
